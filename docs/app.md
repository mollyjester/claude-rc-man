# rcman as an app

rcman can be installed as an app on your computer, phone or tablet. The app is the same rcman
you use in the browser: it opens in a window of its own, without the browser's address bar,
with rcman's name and icon. It needs a connection to rcman, as the browser does, and nothing
works offline.

## Installing it

Sign in to rcman in the browser first, then:

- **Chrome or Edge on a computer**: choose the install icon at the right of the address bar, or
  the browser's menu item that installs rcman as an app (in Edge, under **Apps**).
- **Chrome on Android**: open the menu and choose **Add to home screen** (or **Install app**),
  then **Install**.
- **Safari on an iPhone or iPad**: choose the Share button and **Add to Home Screen**, then
  **Add**.

The app then opens from the start menu, the dock or the home screen, on the Workspaces page.
Links to claude.ai open in the browser or the Claude app, not in rcman's window. To remove the
app, remove it as you would any other app on that device.

## Signing in again

When your sign-in ends, the page says so and offers **Sign in again**. It takes you through the
Google sign-in and back to the page you were on.

A link to a page of rcman that you open while signed out takes you to that page after the
sign-in, but keeps only the first of the settings after its `?`: the workspace it named, say,
but not the tab (`?workspace=…&tab=terminal` comes back as `?workspace=…`).
